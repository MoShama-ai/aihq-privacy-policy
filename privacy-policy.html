Privacy Policy - AI-HQ Marketing

Effective date: 20 November 2026

AI-HQ Marketing ("the App") is a private tool used by its owner to manage and publish content on the owner's own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts through the Meta Platform. It is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

When you log in with Facebook, the App receives only the data covered by the permissions you approve, such as your name, user ID, the Pages and Instagram accounts you manage, access tokens, and post or insight data for those accounts.

How we use it

This data is used only to publish and schedule posts, read engagement and performance metrics, and operate the App's features for the account owner. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored on the owner's own systems with reasonable security measures and kept only as long as needed to run the App. Tokens can be revoked at any time from your Facebook settings.

Data deletion

To delete your data:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find "AI-HQ Marketing" and click Remove. This revokes the App's access.
  2. Email the address below with the subject "Data deletion request". All data the App holds about you will be deleted within 30 days and we will confirm by email.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
